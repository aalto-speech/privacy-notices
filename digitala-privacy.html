1. Purpose of the Research Study and Data Processing

We are conducting a research study on automatic speech recognition, automated speaking assessment, and related natural language processing technologies for Finnish language learning.

The purpose of the study is to develop, evaluate, and improve systems that can process spoken Finnish and provide automatic speaking assessment. This may include, for example, estimating aspects of speaking performance such as proficiency, pronunciation, fluency, accuracy, and range.

The research may use speech recordings, transcriptions, annotations, model-generated assessment results, and relevant speaker background information. These data are used to improve automatic speech recognition and automated speaking assessment models, and to better understand how such systems can support Finnish language learning.

The data controller for this research is Mikko Kurimo and can be contacted at mikko.kurrimo@aalto.fi

2. Personal Data Undergoing Processing

The data processed in this study may include:

  • Audio recordings of spoken Finnish
  • Transcriptions or annotations of the recordings
  • Automated speaking assessment results, such as proficiency, pronunciation, fluency, accuracy, and range scores
  • Language skill level or self-assessed Finnish level
  • User feedback about the assessment results or app experience
  • Background information, such as age group, gender, native language, other languages, and time spent learning Finnish
  • Technical identifiers, such as a pseudonymous user ID

The study does not aim to identify individual participants. Direct identifiers, such as names, are not required for the research dataset unless separately collected for administrative purposes.

3. Data Processing and Pseudonymisation

All personal data processed in this study are used only for the purposes of the research described above.

During analysis, data are processed in a way that reduces the possibility of identifying individual participants. Direct identifiers are removed or replaced with pseudonymous identifiers, such as random user IDs.

Research results will be reported only in a form that does not identify individual participants. No participant’s identity will be disclosed in scientific publications, presentations, or other publicly available research outputs.

4. Legal Basis for Data Processing

The legal basis for processing personal data is scientific research carried out as a task in the public interest.

Where separate consent is requested, for example for optional participation or optional data use, participants may choose whether to provide that consent.

5. Personal Data Sharing

Personal data processed in the study will not be shared with third parties unless this is necessary for the research and permitted under applicable data protection rules.

If data are shared with research collaborators, the data will be shared only in a secure and controlled manner, and only to the extent necessary for the research purposes.

Public research outputs will not contain information that directly identifies individual participants.

6. International Data Transfers

Research data containing personal data will not be transferred outside the European Union or European Economic Area, or to international organisations, unless appropriate safeguards required by data protection law are in place.

7. Data Storage and Protection

Personal data are stored and processed in secure IT systems approved for research use.

Access to the data is restricted to authorised research team members and necessary technical or administrative personnel. Access is protected by user-specific credentials and appropriate security measures.

Audio recordings and assessment data are handled securely, and access is limited to people who need the data for the research.

8. Retention and Deletion of Personal Data

Research data containing personal data may be retained for further scientific research in the same research area or in related research areas that support the purpose of this study.

Research data containing personal data will be deleted after five (5) years from the last publication in which the data have been used, unless a shorter retention period is specified by the project or required by law.

If participants request deletion of their data, the research team will process the request according to applicable data protection rules. In some cases, participants may need to provide their pseudonymous user ID so that their data can be located.

9. Rights of the Research Participant

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), data subjects may have the right to:

  • receive information about the processing of their personal data
  • access personal data collected and processed about them
  • request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • request restriction of processing
  • object to the processing of personal data
  • request deletion of personal data, if the conditions under GDPR Article 17 are met

Some rights may be limited if the data can no longer be linked to a specific individual, or if processing is necessary for scientific research or archiving purposes in the public interest under GDPR Article 89.

If identification is no longer possible, the research team may not be able to access, correct, or delete a specific participant’s data unless the participant can provide additional information, such as their pseudonymous user ID.
